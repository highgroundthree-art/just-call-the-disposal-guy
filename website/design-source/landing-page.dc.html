The rules

  • Flat rate, everything included. Install, haul-away, old unit and packaging. No surprises.
  • 1-year leak-free guarantee. If our install leaks within a year, we come back and fix it free. The manufacturer's in-home warranty covers the unit itself.
  • Plug-in disposals only, for now. If your current unit is hardwired, we can't take the job yet.
  • Empty cabinet required. Everything out from under the sink before we arrive. It keeps your price this low.
  • Under-sink photos required. It protects you from surprises and us from wasted trips.
  • Deposits. Full refund if you cancel 48+ hours before your slot. Inside 48 hours, or once your disposal has been delivered, the $100 is non-refundable and we collect the unopened unit.
  • Scheduling. We install the week after your disposal arrives. Want it sooner? You're automatically on our move-up list: if a slot opens, we text you first.
  • Rescheduling. Free once with 24 hours notice, and always free if your unit ships late.
  • Payment. The balance is charged to your card on file when the job is done.
